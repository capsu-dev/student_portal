About the Application

The CAPSU Student Portal is a mobile application developed for Capiz State University students. It provides students with quick access to their academic and student information using their Android devices.

The application uses the student's official CAPSU account to access available university services and information. It is designed to make commonly used student services easier to access through a simple and mobile-friendly interface.

Main Navigation

Home

The Home page provides an overview of the student's account, university notices, announcements, and quick access to commonly used services.

Quick Access

Quick Access provides shortcuts to important student services such as the profile, grades, schedule, payment ledger, and other available services.

Profile

Displays the student's profile information and profile picture.

Academic Information

Displays academic information such as the student's program, campus, year level, section, and other available academic details.

Personal Information

Displays the student's personal information registered in the university system.

Grades

Allows students to view their academic grades for their enrolled or completed subjects.

Schedule

Displays the student's class schedule, including subjects, instructors, schedules, and other available class information.

Payment Ledger

Allows students to view their payment records and account balance based on the information provided by the university system.

University Notices

Provides students with university announcements, notices, and other important information.

Calendar

Provides information about academic activities, university events, and other important dates.

Account and Security

The application requires a valid CAPSU student account to access student services.

Students should keep their account credentials private and should not share their login information with others.

The application also uses session management to help protect student accounts when the application is not actively being used.

Important Information

The information displayed in the application comes from authorized Capiz State University systems. The available information and services may depend on the student's account and the services currently provided by the university.

For concerns regarding student information, academic records, enrollment, payments, or account access, students should contact the appropriate CAPSU office.
