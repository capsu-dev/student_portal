About CAPSU Student Portal

Official mobile application for Capiz State University students.

About the Application

The CAPSU Student Portal is a mobile application developed for students of Capiz State University. It provides a convenient way for students to access their university information and available student services using an Android mobile device.

The application is designed to provide students with quick access to commonly needed information such as their student profile, personal information, academic information, grades, class schedule, payment records, university notices, and important academic dates.

Instead of accessing different services through a web browser, students can use the mobile application as a central place for checking their student information. The interface is designed to be simple and easy to navigate, allowing students to find the information they need with fewer steps.

After signing in using their official CAPSU student account, students can access the services available to their account. Information shown in the application is based on the records and services provided by the university system.

The application also provides university notices and other important information to help students stay updated with announcements, academic activities, events, and other university-related updates.

The CAPSU Student Portal is intended for officially enrolled students of Capiz State University. A valid student account is required to access the application's student services.

The application is developed to support easier access to student information and services while providing a simple mobile experience for CAPSU students. The available features may be updated as additional university services are made available.

Main Navigation

The CAPSU Student Portal is organized into different sections to make it easier for students to find their academic and personal information. The main navigation provides access to the most commonly used student services and university information.

Home

The Home page is the main screen of the application. It gives students a quick view of important information after signing in. Students can see their basic account information, university notices, announcements, and other information that may require their attention. The Home page also provides quick access to frequently used student services so that students do not need to go through several menus to find them.

Quick Access

Quick Access provides shortcuts to the main services available in the application. From this section, students can quickly open their profile, grades, class schedule, payment ledger, personal information, academic information, and other available services. It is designed to make frequently used features easier to reach from the Home page.

Profile

The Profile section provides an overview of the student's account and registered information. Students can view their profile picture and basic student details. It serves as a central place where students can check the information associated with their CAPSU student account.

Academic Information

The Academic Information section contains information related to the student's academic status at Capiz State University. This may include the student's program, campus, year level, section, student classification, and other academic details provided by the university system. Students can use this section to check their current academic information without needing to access a separate system.

Personal Information

The Personal Information section displays the student's personal details registered in the university system. Depending on the information available in the student's account, this may include contact details and other basic personal information. Students can use this section to review the information associated with their student record.

Grades

The Grades section allows students to view their academic grades using their mobile device. Students can check the grades recorded for their subjects and review their academic performance without having to use a desktop computer. The information displayed depends on the records available from the university system.

Schedule

The Schedule section allows students to view their current class schedule. It provides information about their subjects, instructors, class times, and other schedule details available from the university system. Students can use this section to check their classes while on campus or while away from a computer.

Payment Ledger

The Payment Ledger section allows students to review their financial records available in the university system. Students can check recorded payments, account transactions, and their current account balance when the information is available. This provides students with a convenient way to monitor their payment records through their mobile device.

University Notices

The University Notices section provides students with important announcements and information from Capiz State University. These notices may contain updates, reminders, activities, schedules, or other information relevant to students. Students are encouraged to regularly check this section for new announcements.

Calendar

The Calendar section provides information about important academic dates and university activities. Students can use it to check upcoming events, academic activities, and other scheduled activities provided by the university. It can help students keep track of important dates throughout the academic year.

Forms & Documents

The Forms & Documents section provides access to commonly requested student documents and forms when they are available through the university system. Instead of searching for these documents separately, students can access the available files directly through the application and download them to their mobile device for future use.

Account and Security

The application requires a valid CAPSU student account to access student services.

Students should keep their account credentials private and should not share their login information with others.

The application also uses session management to help protect student accounts when the application is not actively being used.

Important Information

The information displayed in the application comes from authorized Capiz State University systems. The available information and services may depend on the student's account and the services currently provided by the university.

For concerns regarding student information, academic records, enrollment, payments, or account access, students should contact the appropriate CAPSU office.
