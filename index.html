About RNP Trading
Realem Native Products Trading • Since 2000

Realem Native Products Trading (RNP Trading) was established in 2000 by spouses Lorna and Ramonito Diergos with an initial capital of ₱3,000. The business started by producing small, made-to-order furniture such as doors and cabinets, with family members later joining to help meet growing customer demand from churches, schools, and households. Today, the business is managed by Engr. Lemuel E. Diergos and specializes in locally crafted furniture, particularly products made from Madre de Cacao wood. REALEM continues to combine traditional craftsmanship, quality materials, and innovative designs to create durable and distinctive furniture.
